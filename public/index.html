About CampusBite

“Skip the queue. Grab your bite.”

CampusBite was created to address one of the most common everyday frustrations of college students: spending 15 to 20 minutes standing in packed canteen queues during short 10-minute lecture breaks.

With CampusBite, students can browse the daily freshly prepared canteen menu, customize their order, submit their roll number, and monitor real-time kitchen preparation status right from their smartphone. When your food is hot and packed, simply walk to the pickup counter and present your order number.

📍 Campus Canteen Counter

Ground Floor, Student Activity Center (SAC Building), Campus Main Square.

🕒 Operating Hours

Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM – 5:30 PM (Kitchen closes at 5:00 PM).
